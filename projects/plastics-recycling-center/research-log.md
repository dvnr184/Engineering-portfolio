#### Finding 3: Why Recycling Contamination is Important

**What I found:**  
Recycling contamination is a big problem. It is defined as something that can degrade other materials or the recycling process. Things like food, liquids, and tissues are considered contaminants. These things can put entire loads of recycling to waste by making them unrecyclable and turning them into heaps of trash.

This matters because this defines a problem that needs to be dealt with throughout the project because it is considered a roadblock
